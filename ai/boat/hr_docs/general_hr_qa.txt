# WORKING HOURS

Q: What are the working hours?
A: Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break.

Q: Is there a probation period?
A: Yes, new employees have a 3-month probation period, with performance reviews at 30, 60, and 90 days.

Q: What is the dress code?
A: We maintain a business casual dress code. Some departments may have specific requirements for safety or client meetings.

Q: Can I work remotely?
A: Flexible remote work may be available depending on your role, up to 2 days per week with manager approval, subject to business needs.
