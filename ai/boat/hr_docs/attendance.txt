# MARKING ATTENDANCE

Q: Where do I mark my attendance?
A: Log in to the HR Portal and go to the "Attendance" section, or use the Connect mobile app.

Q: How do I check in?
A: Log in to the HR Portal/app and click "Check In" at the start of your day.

Q: How do I check out?
A: Log in to the HR Portal/app and click "Check Out" at the end of your day.

Q: What are my work hours?
A: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

Q: What is the grace period for check-in?
A: You can check in until 9:15 AM without being marked late.

# LATE ARRIVAL

Q: When am I considered late?
A: If you check in after 9:15 AM.

Q: What happens if I'm late?
A: • 9:15 AM – 10:00 AM = Marked late
• After 10:00 AM = Marked late (may require manager approval)
• After 12:00 PM = Marked as half day
Multiple late check-ins may affect your attendance record.

Q: What if I check in after 10 AM?
A: Checking in after 10:00 AM is marked as late in your attendance record. Please inform your manager if this happens regularly.

Q: What if I check in after 12 PM?
A: Checking in after 12:00 PM is marked as a half day, not just late. Contact your manager and, if needed, apply for leave to cover the missed half.

Q: Is there a half day rule for late attendance?
A: Yes — if you check in after 12:00 PM, your attendance for that day is marked as a half day.

Q: How do I report being late?
A: HR Portal > "Report Late Arrival" > Select reason > Submit, and inform your manager.

# WORK FROM HOME

Q: How do I mark attendance for work from home?
A: HR Portal > Select "Work From Home" > Check in at 9:00 AM > Check out at 5:00 PM, same as in-office attendance.

# CHECK ATTENDANCE

Q: How do I check my attendance record?
A: HR Portal > "Attendance History" or "Summary Report".

Q: How many days was I present this month?
A: Check HR Portal > "Summary Report" for a full breakdown.

Q: How many times was I late?
A: HR Portal > "Summary Report" lists all late entries.

# RAMADAN / SPECIAL SCHEDULES

Q: Do attendance timings change during Ramadan?
A: Yes, reduced working hours typically apply during Ramadan as per company policy each year. Check the HR Portal announcement or contact HR for the exact schedule.
