# APPLYING FOR LEAVE

Q: How do I apply for leave?
A: Log in to the HR Portal > "Leave Management" > Pick dates > Select leave type > Add reason > Submit.

Q: Where do I apply for leave?
A: Go to the HR Portal > "Leave Management" section.

Q: How to request time off?
A: HR Portal > Leave Management > Fill details > Submit.

# LEAVE LIMITS

Q: How many leaves do I get per month?
A: You are entitled to 2 casual leaves per month, which is 24 leaves per year.

Q: Can I take more than 2 leaves in a month?
A: Yes, but it requires your manager's special approval.

Q: How many leaves can I carry forward?
A: Unused casual leaves can be carried forward, up to a maximum of 12 leaves per year.

# NOTICE PERIOD

Q: How many days before should I apply for leave?
A: Planned leave requires 2 days advance notice. Emergency or sick leave can be applied for on the same day.

Q: What about last-minute leave?
A: Inform your manager immediately, then apply in the HR Portal as soon as possible.

# SICK LEAVE

Q: How do I take sick leave?
A: HR Portal > Select "Sick Leave" > Add reason > Submit. Sick leave counts against your monthly casual leave quota.

Q: Is a doctor's note required for sick leave?
A: A medical certificate is required if sick leave exceeds 3 days.

Q: Can I take sick leave without a medical certificate?
A: Yes, for 1-3 days without one, provided it's within your casual leave balance.

# EMERGENCY LEAVE

Q: What do I do for a sudden emergency leave?
A: 1. Call or message your manager right away
2. Apply in the HR Portal, tagging it as "Emergency"
3. Submit any supporting proof within 24 hours if applicable

Q: Family emergency leave?
A: Inform your manager first, then apply as emergency leave in the HR Portal.

# MEDICAL LEAVE

Q: What if I need long-term medical leave?
A: Contact HR directly, submit a medical leave request with your doctor's note and supporting documents.

# BEREAVEMENT LEAVE

Q: Is there leave for a death in the family?
A: Yes, 3-5 days of paid bereavement leave is provided. Inform HR of your relationship to the deceased.

# RELIGIOUS AND FESTIVAL LEAVE

Q: Is there leave for Hajj?
A: Yes, eligible employees can apply for Hajj leave once during their service, subject to HR approval and company policy. Apply well in advance through HR.

Q: Do working hours change during Ramadan?
A: Yes, working hours are typically reduced during Ramadan as per company policy. Check the HR Portal or contact HR for the exact schedule each year.

Q: Are Eid holidays paid?
A: Yes, Eid-ul-Fitr and Eid-ul-Adha holidays are paid, following the official government-announced holiday schedule.

# UNPAID LEAVE

Q: What if I need leave but have no balance left?
A: You can request Leave Without Pay (LWP), which requires manager approval.

Q: How do I apply for unpaid leave?
A: Ask your manager and HR at least 7 days before, subject to business/work needs.

# DURING LEAVE

Q: Do my benefits continue during leave?
A: Yes, your benefits continue as normal during approved leave.

Q: Can I cancel an approved leave?
A: HR Portal > "My Leaves" > Select the leave > "Cancel", then inform your manager.

Q: Can I extend my leave?
A: Apply for an extension in the HR Portal and inform your manager. Extension is subject to approval.

# RETURN FROM LEAVE

Q: What do I do on my first day back from leave?
A: Inform your manager you're back and check your attendance status in the HR Portal.

Q: Do I need anything to return from sick leave?
A: If your sick leave was more than 3 days, submit a medical clearance/fitness certificate.

# LEAVE BALANCE

Q: How do I check my leave balance?
A: HR Portal > "Leave Balance" section shows your total, used, and remaining leaves.

Q: Who approves my leave?
