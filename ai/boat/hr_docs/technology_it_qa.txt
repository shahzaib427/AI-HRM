# ACCOUNT & PASSWORD

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password." Enter your registered email and you'll receive a reset link that expires within 24 hours.

Q: I didn't receive the password reset email. What should I do?
A: Check your spam/junk folder first. If it still hasn't arrived after 10 minutes, contact IT support at shahzaibnaseem3169@icloud.com or 03134750548.

Q: What are the password requirements?
A: Passwords must be at least 12 characters long, including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters. Passwords expire every 90 days.

Q: What should I do if I'm locked out of my account?
A: After multiple failed login attempts, your account locks for 30 minutes. Wait and try again, or use "Forgot Password." For urgent help, contact IT at shahzaibnaseem3169@icloud.com.

Q: How do I enable two-factor authentication?
A: Go to Security Settings in your account profile, select "Enable 2FA," and follow the prompts.
