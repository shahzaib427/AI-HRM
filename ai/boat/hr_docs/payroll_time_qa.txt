# DEDUCTIONS

Q: What is deducted from my salary?
A: Income tax as per FBR slabs, EOBI contribution, and Provident Fund contribution (if enrolled), plus any benefit deductions you've opted into.

Q: Why is income tax deducted?
A: Income tax is deducted and deposited with FBR (Federal Board of Revenue) as required by law, based on your applicable tax slab.

Q: What is EOBI?
A: EOBI (Employees' Old-Age Benefits Institution) is a government-mandated old-age benefit scheme. The company contributes to your EOBI account as required by law.

Q: What is Provident Fund?
A: Provident Fund is a retirement savings benefit where both you and the company contribute a percentage of your basic salary monthly. Ask HR about your eligibility and contribution rate.

Q: What is Gratuity?
A: Gratuity is a lump-sum payment made on completion of a qualifying period of service or on leaving the company, as per company policy and labor law.

# OVERTIME

Q: How is overtime calculated?
A: Overtime is paid at 1.5 times your normal hourly rate for approved hours worked beyond your standard schedule.

Q: How do I request overtime approval?
A: Submit your request in the time-tracking system at least 24 hours in advance and get manager approval.
